## Creative programme
Three streams are maintained together: **Story**, **CYOD architecture**, and **World Bible**. Book One's three-act structure and minimalist wrist-choice opening are approved as *design architecture*. Actual manuscript chapters, final illustrations, exact world-laws, protagonist identity, and the solution to ZETA's larger mystery are not yet approved.

W001 and W002 are closed; W003 is prepared, **not executed**. The accepted Book One narrative is wrongful mathematical exile → regional investigation/refutation → return and vindication, with unresolved agency foreshadowing. Do not promote proposals into canon without explicit owner approval. This repository is an internal creative archive, not an announcement that the novel is written.
