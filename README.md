## Creative programme
Three streams are maintained together: **Story**, **CYOD architecture**, and **World Bible**. Book One's three-act structure and minimalist wrist-choice opening are approved as *design architecture*. Actual manuscript chapters, final illustrations, exact world-laws, protagonist identity, and the solution to ZETA's larger mystery are not yet approved.

W001–W003 are closed; W004 is prepared, **not executed**. The accepted Book One narrative is wrongful mathematical exile → regional investigation/refutation → return and vindication, with unresolved agency foreshadowing. The print-first journey blends River (Act I), Atlas (Act II) and Living Book (Act III), with authored page/paragraph navigation, guided discovery and light choice-led danger rather than RPG combat or provisions. Reader-facing novel prose does not explicitly name maths/mathematics. Do not promote proposals into canon without explicit owner approval. This repository is an internal creative archive, not an announcement that the novel is written.
