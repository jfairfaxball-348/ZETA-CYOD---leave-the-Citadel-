## Important invariants
1. **Print 102/203/304/2 are draft page jumps**, **not N-#### node IDs**.
2. The initial mark selection persists; there is no free Act II destination vote in the first version of the structure.
3. Initial choice **does not alter shared Act I's text/route**; it may shape what the character already knows from their initial explanatory paragraph.
4. Shared Act I may contain additional genuine choices (exact consequences remain OPEN), and must serve character, story and discovery.
5. Act II is three substantial different experiences, each producing a distinct refutation of a false exile judgement by its conclusion. Act III returns to the Citadel for confrontation/vindication with route-conditioned consequences and an unexplained agency anomaly; this is thematic structure, not extra graph nodes.

## W003 approved navigation layers (design, not implemented)

- **Act I / River:** mostly forward choice-forks and reconvergence, keeping the wrist-mark-selected region dormant throughout shared Act I.
- **Act II / Atlas:** mark-determined region with more exploration, optional loops, reconvergence, meaningful detours and discoveries; route-specific refuting evidence is required by act end.
- **Act III / Living Book:** more ambitious authored numerical/physical-book interactions, compatible with return to the Citadel, route-conditioned consequences and the single unexplained agency anomaly.
- **Print navigation:** reader may jump to page numbers, ordered paragraph labels or bounded preauthored numeric destinations. Physical page, reader-visible passage label and stable internal node ID are DISTINCT. Paragraphs can be numerically ordered in print while reading order jumps.
- **Address safety:** protect plot gateways, endings and mandatory revelations against accidental numeric access; validate valid inputs, edges, loops, re-entry, route selection, reachable required evidence and terminal vindication. None of these checks has been run because nodes do not yet exist.
- **Reader experience:** this remains a choice-led novel, not a combat/item/provision/roll-for-luck RPG. Any random or deterministic traversal must serve story and remain playable without specialist knowledge/equipment.

**Collatz-labelled button, hailstone jumps, random walks, modulo puzzles, hidden routes and special per-region rules are PROPOSALS, not installed mechanics.**
