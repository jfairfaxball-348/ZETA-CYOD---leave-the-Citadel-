# CYOD — W001 frozen opening and delayed regional selection

**Status: CANON at the approved *architecture* level (DEC-0007/0008).** Not a commissioned opening chapter, final copy edit, picture set, executable game state model or printer-ready pagination.

## Minimal opening concept
The book starts directly, with *no narrative context before the choice* beyond:

> “…leave The Citadel.”
>
> That is what you must do.
>
> You look down at your right wrist.
>
> What do you see?

The above quotes record the owner's preferred **working words and sequence**, not a final authorized prose passage. The answer choices should appear as **three images** rather than written descriptions.

| Choice image (to create later) | Printed jump (draft) | Mathematically themed region for Act II | Opening passage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A **single-digit numeral** (specific digit OPEN) | **102** | Number-theory territory (Citadel is within it, but this route does not mean staying in Citadel) | A **specific arithmetic/number-theory basis** for necessary departure (OPEN) |

**W002 narrative condition (DEC-0011/0012):** The three opening claims must feel mathematically compelling *to the Citadel*, but each is eventually shown to be an unjustified basis for exile. Their different errors are still OPEN and the reader should not know the eventual refutation immediately. Do not assert that a valid real-world theorem is false.

Each of the three early passages must:
1. Be a **single, substantive block** furnishing the corresponding mathematical reason that the protagonist must leave.
2. End with **one and only one** continuation: **Turn to page 2**.
3. Set the persistent opening selection (conceptually; final print implementation open). Do **not** require a second Act II destination choice.

From draft page **2**, Act I is a shared adventure outside the Citadel. The selected mark is **not used to change the shared Act I narrative**. It becomes consequential at the Act I/II transition, where the protagonist enters/lives in the corresponding region. Act III reconverges in destination at the Citadel without erasing route-specific consequences.

- The reader must be able to remember the initial mark; the physical wrist depiction may itself serve as the visible reminder, with more explicit print tracking considered only if useful.

## W010 approved staging refinement (DEC-0021–0023)
The instruction is **remembered speech** from Chapter Elder Luiz, the approximately 25-year-old apprentice player's mentor. The mark choice selects one of three **recollections of Luiz's explanation**; it does not mean that a new voice speaks at the gate. All three versions return to the same present-day exit scene: the player alone at the Citadel's outermost door, unable to return through the shut inner door, with an open doorway leading out into the surrounding number-theory territory. Narration is second person and briskly descriptive. These refinements change no existing printed placeholder destinations or Act II-routing design.

## What remains OPEN
Whether the opening choice **reveals** a pre-existing alleged necessity or **causes** its institutional enforcement; the details of Luiz's earlier conversation; what specifically compels departure despite the false claim; the object's reality, possible changing marks, which assumptions fail, and how three regional traditions relate. In particular, never infer that the single digit is **7** or the geometric image is a mere polyhedron.
