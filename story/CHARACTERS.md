# Book One — Characters and relationships

**OPEN:** No protagonist, companion, mentor, antagonist, faction representative or point-of-view arrangement is established. Character names, motives and arcs must be explored and approved in later workshops.

## Working character dossier format (template only)
For each *future* character, record:
- Stable character ID (allocate only on actual addition), name and **status**.
- Role/voice and desire; immediate obstacle and emotional stakes.
- Knowledge, misbeliefs and relationship to the Citadel or other places.
- How decisions may change relationships without breaking continuity.
- Appearance across nodes and any endings; source workshop and DEC-ID when canon.

**PROPOSAL only, not selected:** an apprentice cartographer or an Archivist AI. Neither is implied by the working title or the architecture.
