## Questions and editorial criteria
- Why is leaving the Citadel emotionally necessary, alluring, risky or complicated?
- What would make the story compelling even to readers with little mathematical background?
- What conflict drives each branch beyond discovering scenery?
- Which themes are dramatized through decisions and consequences, rather than explained?
- What is the intended narrative voice, tempo and balance of wonder and character?
- How do routes converge, diverge and satisfy the reader without negating earlier choices?
