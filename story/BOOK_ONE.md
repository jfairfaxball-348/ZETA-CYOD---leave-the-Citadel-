# Book One — *Leave the Citadel* (working title)

**CANON — DEC-0003:** First ZETA work uses Choose Your Own Destiny (CYOD) reader choices.
**CANON (design) — DEC-0006–0010:** W001 freezes the act-level structure and delayed wrist-mark mechanism below; not prose, final pagination, character traits, exact lore or endings.

## Opening
- Absolutely minimal pre-choice context: an instruction beginning **“...leave The Citadel”** and a look down at the protagonist's **right wrist**; then **three illustrated options**. No introductory worldbuilding or descriptive option labels are required.
- Illustrations: single digit → draft page **102**; topologically suggestive knot or interlocking surface → **203**; binomial symbol `\binom{n}{k}` → **304**. These are temporary *page destinations*, not final node numbers.
- Each choice passage delivers one **different mathematically grounded reason** departure is obligatory, then its only option is **turn to page 2**.
- **CANON (W002):** Each route's asserted mathematical necessity is ultimately *unjustified*, despite being presented as compulsory in the opening; the precise flawed premise/deception and how departure is enforced remain OPEN. The protagonist departs the Citadel; the converged story begins Act I. Luiz speaks the remembered instruction; the precise judgements, how they function, and the marks' exact nature remain OPEN.

## W010 approved voice and opening staging (DEC-0021–0023)
- **Voice:** Second-person (`you`) throughout reader-facing prose; concise, descriptive, forward-moving gamebook narration. Avoid extended psychological introspection or fixed first-person narration.
- **Player:** A young apprentice of approximately 25. The player's name, gender, detailed apprenticeship and personal backstory remain open.
- **Chapter Elder Luiz:** The player's trusted mentor, who earlier told them to “...leave The Citadel.” The opening words are a **memory**, not speech arriving at the gate. Each wrist-mark branch can elaborate a different version of that conversation and its judgement; the three exact judgements remain OPEN.
- **Present scene:** The player stands alone near the **outermost Citadel exit**, inward door shut, outward door standing open into the surrounding number-theory territory. The region's fantasy name remains undecided; **Arithen** is a proposal only. Each memory branch still rejoins the single shared page 2; no content for the branch is approved yet.

## W003 approved experience and print-facing voice
**CANON (design) — DEC-0016–0020:** Make the three acts a single, coherent hybrid gamebook: directed River exploration in Act I, richer Atlas interconnections in Act II, and more inventive Living Book navigation in Act III. Use a mix of physical page jumps and ascending-numbered paragraph labels; numeric destinations must be explicitly authored and protected against plot bypasses. Concrete puzzles, numbers and locations remain OPEN. The novel's reader-facing prose and in-world instructions must never explicitly use “maths” or “mathematics”. Danger and consequences come primarily from meaningful choices, not dice-driven combat, RPG equipment, provisions or hit-point/luck stats. Guided discovery trumps expository rules teaching.

## Creative promise
- **CANON — DEC-0010:** The dominant emotional experience begins as unsettling suspicion that the protagonist's accepted worldview may be wrong.
- Mathematical ideas underpin lived fiction rather than provide lessons or clichés.
- The three regions can feel deeply unlike one another, while the wider ZETA mythology connects them in ways not yet resolved.
- **OPEN:** Protagonist's name, appearance, gender, specific apprenticeship, personhood, agency and other relationships/stakes. The antagonists' motives, individual knowledge, alleged proofs and possible actual constraints on choice are also OPEN.
- **PROPOSAL only:** Mathematical balance, proof, contradiction, constraint and simulation theory as possible explanatory inspirations; no specific mechanism accepted.
