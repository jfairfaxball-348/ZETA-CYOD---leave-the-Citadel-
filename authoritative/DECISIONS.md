## Explicitly not canonized
The ultimate mystery, including **simulation theory** or predetermination as truth; the precise mathematical justifications for exile and how they are refuted (balance, contradiction, proof, constraints are proposals); the future π-computation/pattern-discovery scenario (PROPOSAL, not commissioned); a named or gendered protagonist; a numeral such as 7; the exact topological object; permanent branding/tattoo vs other physical representation; any particular road scenes, factions, nations, laws, endings, final printing page numbers, or full story-node implementation.

When changing an approved item, add a new DEC-ID, preserve this record, annotate supersession and reconcile story/lore/CYOD references.
