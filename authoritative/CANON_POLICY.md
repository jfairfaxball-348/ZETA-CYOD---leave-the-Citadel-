# ZETA — Canon, uncertainty and revisions

Every substantial creative assertion must visibly carry one status:

| Status | Meaning | Publication/editorial treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **CANON** | Explicitly owner-approved at stated specificity. | Authoritative until explicitly superseded. |
| **PROVISIONAL** | Owner-affirmed in principle or tentatively favoured, still revisable. | Useful design direction; not a fixed world fact. |
| **PROPOSAL** | Suggested (by owner, editor or AI) without acceptance. | Keep alternatives open; never present as established. |
| **OPEN** | A question or unresolved conflict. | No implied answer. |
| **REJECTED** | Explicitly considered and discarded. | Preserve for decision history; do not quietly revive as fact. |

**Permission boundary:** Silence, repetition, lack of objection or AI recommendation do not create CANON. Explicit owner approval must identify what is approved. An instruction to create *infrastructure* is not permission to canonize fictional events.

## Working rules
- Put a status next to the claim or in a clearly scoped section heading; record material canon approval in [DECISIONS](DECISIONS.md).
- An approved statement may be **narrow**: "the Citadel is part of ZETA" does not establish its architecture, origins, citizens or power.
- Treat provisional place examples, characters, route counts and speculative events as *proposals*, unless the owner has actually accepted them.
- Fictional laws, metaphor and invented mathematical effects are not assertions that any real conjecture was proved/disproved. Avoid implying a resolution to the Riemann Hypothesis or other mathematical research.
- A world fact, what an in-world character believes, and what the reader is told are three different things. Tag each where ambiguity matters.
- If contradictory claims emerge, log the issue in [OPEN_QUESTIONS](OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) and request resolution.
- When changing canon, preserve the superseded DEC-ID, log the new one and reconcile [World Bible](../lore/WORLD_BIBLE.md), [Story](../story/BOOK_ONE.md), [CYOD graph](../cyod/STORY_GRAPH.md) and [revelation budget](../story/REVELATION_BUDGET.md) as affected.

## Prior exploratory suggestions — not canon
**PROPOSAL:** disappearing bridges, an apprentice cartographer, an Archivist AI, specified factions, exactly three routes and particular endings. They are not default future requirements.
