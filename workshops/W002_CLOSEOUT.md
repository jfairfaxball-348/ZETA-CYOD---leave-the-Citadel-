# W002 — Why Must They Leave? Owner-approved creative closeout

**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** CLOSED / FROZEN AT STATED SCOPE · **Source:** owner-led W002 conversation following W001; owner authorized repository commit/merge. **Not a chapter, proof, node graph or final lore solution.**

## 1. Inherited W001 foundations (unchanged)
The first story words begin with “...leave The Citadel”, a right-wrist observation and three **illustrated** options: a single digit (draft page 102; number-theory territory), knot/interlocking topological form (203; Topologia), and binomial coefficient (304; Combinatorium). Each short early passage gives an apparently compelling mathematical reason to depart, with its *only* continuation “Turn to page 2”. The paths join the **same Act I**, outside the Citadel in the number-theory territory; the initial selection is remembered and determines the Act II region. Act III returns to the Citadel with route-sensitive effects. These are draft print references, not final pagination or node IDs. The protagonist's identity remains open. [DEC-0005–0010](../authoritative/DECISIONS.md).

## 2. W002 newly approved — the story's engine
- **CANON (design) — DEC-0011:** The emotional and structural arc is **Act I: Exile → Act II: Exploration/investigation → Act III: Return and vindication**. The protagonist does not set out merely to visit strange lands: they want to challenge the grounds of exile.
- **CANON (design) — DEC-0012:** **The Citadel is wrong regardless of which mark the reader saw.** The three accusations/explanations are different, each mathematically framed and ultimately unjustified. Their precise logic and refutations remain OPEN. By the close of the route-specific Act II, the protagonist discovers evidence sufficient to challenge that route's judgement. Act III brings the challenge home to the authorities.
- **CANON (antagonists) — DEC-0013:** Those responsible for the exile are the book's antagonists, intentionally participating in deception or an unjustified ruling. It is *not* simply a sincere administrative arithmetic mistake. Who they are, why they do it and how many know each relevant fact remain OPEN.
- **CANON (thematic design) — DEC-0014:** Most antagonists believe they are making free choices; a few may privately suspect something else. The series-level question is whether they chose to act or were constrained in ways even they cannot understand. This does NOT establish that external control or predetermination exists, nor excuse their actions.
- **CANON (foreshadowing design) — DEC-0015:** Include one unmistakably strange, still unexplained incident in Act III during/around the return and confrontation, hinting that an antagonist's agency may not be wholly voluntary. It should not eclipse the protagonist's local vindication or solve ZETA's cosmology.

The *false claim* is an in-world judgement or faulty application of assumptions, **not a failed valid theorem of real mathematics**. The opening's alleged mathematical necessities make departure institutionally compulsory even if the grounds are false; how compulsion is exerted remains open.

## 3. Three mathematical errors — proposals only
The following are productive examples, **NOT canonized reasons** and not fixed to be used:
- **Number theory:** a rule about order, divisibility, periodicity or invariant is applied outside the conditions that make it valid.
- **Topology:** an alleged impossibility of connection/untangling or spatial structure relies on a false assumption about the environment or permissible transformations.
- **Combinatorics:** an official exhaustive argument quietly excludes a permitted arrangement, configuration or future.
Crucially, the Citadel is wrong *differently* on each route and each Act II is a substantial regional story, not a math lecture. The mathematics shapes a lived injustice and discovery.

## 4. Wider mystery — a deliberately unresolved question
**CANON as narrative direction, NOT cosmological answer:** Who truly chooses? The antagonists actively perpetuate injustice, usually convinced they chose freely. An Act III anomaly should leave a disquieting question about that freedom. Whether ZETA is simulated, deterministic or subject to a completely different underlying condition is **OPEN**. Book One's local vindication must remain meaningful regardless.

**PROPOSAL / long-term story seed ONLY:** A later ZETA story could involve an extremely powerful device calculating enormous numbers of digits of π and a subtle, reproducible pattern apparently predicting choices. Its form, timing, technology, whether any such pattern exists, and what it means are **unfixed**. Do not mistake patterns in deterministic π digits for evidence of random anomalies or a proof of simulation; an authentic fictional discovery would need independent predictive tests. Avoid plot/imagery/theme duplication of Darren Aronofsky's 1998 film *Pi*. No later book, π territory or plot is commissioned by this record.

## 5. Narrative implications
- The initial mark selects **which purported proof is invalid** and **where one learns to refute it**; the shared Act I does not branch on mark.
- An antagonist can be culpable of deception and sincerely feel free while the story keeps deeper questions of agency open.
- Act III may vindicate the protagonist without making the institution automatically apologise or welcome them back; specific resolutions remain OPEN.
- The emotional escalation is personal injustice → discovery of mistaken assumptions → evidence and confrontation → a final crack in certainty, beyond Book One.
- Preserve further real choices, consequences and relationships for the eventual CYOD architecture; delayed destination is only one fixed choice.
- Printed page references and paragraph/node identifiers are distinct design objects until W003.

## 6. Explicitly still OPEN
1. Who or what issues and enforces “...leave The Citadel”; what compels compliance despite an invalid rationale.
2. Three concrete, credible mathematical accusations and their refutations; whether the wrist mark causes, reveals or signals the judgement.
3. The protagonist's identity, voice, circumstances, companions, antagonists' identities, hierarchy and actual motives.
4. Specific roads/events, per-region evidence, meaningful reader choices, confrontations, outcomes and endings.
5. The exact unambiguous yet unresolved Act III strange incident, and whether antagonists have any actual constraint on their agency.
6. True nature of ZETA. Simulation remains a possible *interpretation*, not established canon.
7. Future π idea is a **proposal**, not a settled plot, theorem or world fact.
