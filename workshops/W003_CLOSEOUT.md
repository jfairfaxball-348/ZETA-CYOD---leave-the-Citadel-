## 2. W003 approvals — precise scope
### DEC-0016 — Hybrid act-level journey
**CANON (design):** Use all three structural families together, with a deliberate act-level emphasis: **Act I — River** (directed forward-moving branches and reconvergence), **Act II — Atlas** (richer interconnected investigation and exploration, revisits and shortcuts), **Act III — Living Book** (increasingly intricate interaction with printed navigation/earlier mechanisms). These are emphases, not separate incompatible systems. Keep every act a compelling story with meaningful decisions and consequences. No fixed topology, nodes or per-act mechanical quotas approved.

### DEC-0017 — Print-first, fully authored hybrid navigation
**CANON (design):** Use a purposeful combination of physical page references, individually numbered passages/paragraphs and occasional specially authored numeric transforms/jumps. All narrative text and legal destinations are authored in advance—not generated dynamically. Physical pages and numbered paragraphs are separate addressing systems; stable internal node IDs are yet another layer. Printed passage labels should appear in numerical order, even if reading jumps around. Provisional opening page references 102/203/304/2 remain provisional physical directions, not ratified final pagination or paragraph IDs.
**Safety/integrity requirement:** Reserve important narrative gateways/revelations/endings from accidental access by permitted number operations. When design stabilises, validate *every authorised input and generated destination*, allowed exit, branch reachability, absence of invalid jumps or involuntary softlocks, and the path-specific evidence requirement. Avoid assuming mathematical closure properties without testing bounded domains. This is a design requirement, not a completed graph verification.

### DEC-0018 — Mathematics without naming it
**CANON (style constraint):** In the **reader-facing published narrative and in-world instructions**, do not explicitly say “maths” or “mathematics”. Make the structures, wonder and discovery felt rather than explained in disciplinary vocabulary. Internal design notes and editorial documentation may use technical terminology. Other specific prohibited words or permitted terms remain subject to prose editing.

### DEC-0019 — Lightweight, choice-led adventure
**CANON (experience constraint):** The work is a branching novel, **not** a Fighting Fantasy-style combat RPG. No routine fight-the-monster dice encounters, sword battles resolved by rolls, test-your-luck checks, equipment loadouts, provisions/supply or hit-point management. Danger and meaningful failure can emerge from apparently simple reader choices, their context and consequences. Probability and physical dice remain available for *purposeful navigation/encounter design*, not default success/failure checks; the reader's meaningful agency must not be replaced by mandatory lucky rolls. The persistent wrist-mark discriminant remains; limited narrative memory/choice consequences are not disallowed by this prohibition on RPG-style bookkeeping.

### DEC-0020 — Guided discovery; increasing sophistication
**CANON (experience direction):** Teach mechanisms through intuitive participation and clues rather than textbook exposition. Let readers experience a simple rule, recognise it, and later exploit what they learned. Layer mechanics over the acts as appropriate, respecting accessibility, pacing and print usability. This approves an approach, **not** a particular mathematical puzzle or actual requirement to solve one to finish.
