## Writing requirements

- Show a real encounter and a decision, not four information-dump monologues.
- Main road: established traveller meets Citadel exile, has a credible reason to be wary; small useful revelation grounded in records, observations, intervals or falsifiable Citadel predictions.
- Wall footprints: someone is trying to regain entry or retrieve something; make motive and physical circumstance plausible, grounded in Citadel investigations and secrecy, but *do not canonize a new institutional law or conspiracy mechanism without owner approval*.
- Let each of four choices **prescriptively give a memorable literal letter** (D, K, R or Y); the letter can simply be told to the reader to remember. No need for four ornate symbol-origin stories. This is the only mandatory difference in clue mechanics for this occasion. Each path may also offer distinct **story knowledge**.
- Second person, brisk, atmospheric and readable; protagonist approx. 25, young apprentice, Luiz mentor. Avoid forced exposition, maths/mathematics in reader-facing text, combat RPG, inventory, attribute or luck mechanics.
- Pairwise and final reconvergence must remain true for all four routes without silently assuming the reader saw the alternative scenes.
- No new illustration generation, UI redesign, world cosmology answer, Act-II full plot, or unrelated infrastructure projects.
