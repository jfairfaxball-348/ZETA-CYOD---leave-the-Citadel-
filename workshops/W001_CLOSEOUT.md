# W001 — The World and the Story: owner-approved foundation freeze

**Date:** 2026-10-29 · **Status:** CLOSED (for this foundational exploration) · **Source:** owner-led W001 conversation · **No prose commissioned.**

## 1. World and tone — approved scope
- ZETA's enormous mathematical universe should allow regions that feel **starkly unlike one another**, potentially like entirely different worlds, rather than one homogenized civilization. Beauty, fantasy, philosophical mathematics and science fiction all matter.
- The most important emotional note for Book One is **the unsettling suspicion that everything the protagonist has been taught about reality may be wrong** (DEC-0010).
- The mathematics should guide characters' *circumstances and constraints*, not appear as gimmicky educational exposition.
- The overarching mystery must remain greater than a single theorem or symbol. **Simulation theory is an intriguing speculative hypothesis only, NOT canon.**

## 2. Geography — approved scope
- The Citadel exists **inside a larger number-theory territory**, but is not the whole territory; its fantasy name is **OPEN**. **Arithen** is one proposal.
- **Topologia** and **the Combinatorium** are the other two regions for Book One's substantive Act II choices. Their final forms, cultures, physics and relative geography are OPEN (DEC-0005).
- Later regions may include others, including infinity-inspired settings, but none added to the first book.

## 3. Book One — approved structure
- **Act I (~first third):** immediate instruction to leave and exit the Citadel. After a three-way short opening detour, the story converges and proceeds on a largely shared journey **outside the Citadel, within number-theory territory**. It is a vessel for learning, encounters, choices and building stakes—not a full number-theory regional deep-dive. A literal *road* is PROPOSAL.
- **Act II (~middle third):** one of three deeply distinct regional narratives. The region is **not chosen afresh here**; it is determined by the opening right-wrist image choice.
- **Act III (~final third):** return to the Citadel for a generally prescribed central resolution, with materially different knowledge, consequences, relationships and potentially endings depending on the second-act path. Broad convergence must never negate meaningful differences (DEC-0006/0009).

## 4. Opening mechanism — approved architecture
- The reader encounters almost no context before the first choice, only **“…leave The Citadel”** as the opening command, the compulsion to obey, and the protagonist noticing the **right wrist**.
- The opening query is **“What do you see?”** It presents **three illustrations rather than prose descriptions**:
  1. A **single-digit numeral** → draft page **102** → number-theory territory in Act II.
  2. An **impossible/topological knot or interlocking surface** → draft page **203** → Topologia in Act II.
  3. **Binomial coefficient** `\binom{n}{k}` → draft page **304** → the Combinatorium in Act II.
- Each corresponding opening passage is one substantial block telling a **different mathematical reason why the protagonist must leave**, then has **only one onward choice, “Turn to page 2”**.
- All enter a *shared Act I* on draft page 2; the mark is filed away and has **no branch effect on Act I**. It returns as the determinant of Act II destination.
- No final printed page assignments, graph IDs, images or explanatory prose are asserted as complete (DEC-0007/0008).

## 5. Candid limits / open creative decisions
- **Why** does the protagonist actually have to leave? Three different math-derived forces (possibly balance, topology, combinatorial constraints, proofs, contradictions) have been raised, none accepted. W002 starts here.
- The speaker, authority and actual compulsory nature of the command, and whether marks **reveal** a pre-existing necessity or **cause** it, all OPEN.
- No protagonist appearance, gender, identity, psychological backstory, companions, regions' physical laws, detailed plot or ending approved.
- No final rendering of topological knot, particular numeral, wrist inscription type or transport established.
- No full graph or prose manuscript created.
- **Simulation theory remains PROPOSAL**. The larger question's answer remains OPEN.

## 6. Creative warning for next workshop
A triple explanation must fit the same shared departure scene **without** making all three variants the exact same explanation with different math terminology. Find dramatic necessities readers understand without lectures; preserve mystery over actual vs believed world-laws. The first choice is *what the protagonist already sees*, not an omniscient menu choosing region. Keep later agency real.
