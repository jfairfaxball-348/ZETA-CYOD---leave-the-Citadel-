## Owner-approved design decisions

1. P-0005 already presents **road to settlement** versus **fresh footprints beside Citadel wall**. Each path will split into **two further options**, producing four different first-clue experiences. The four rejoin locally into road/wall continuations and then reconverge into shared Act I. The complete prose of those four experiences is the next workshop's job.
2. Knowledge/remembered choices, rather than inventory, equipment, statistics or luck, form Book One's distinguishing reader state. Three distinct four-way clue encounters produce a letter, number and colour; one of each on any playthrough.
3. Explicit twelve clue set: **D/K/R/Y**, **7/16/25/34**, **GREEN/AMBER/BLACK/WHITE**. Alphabet-position remainder modulo 7 / digit sum / colour-name length resolve to **4/7/5** for all 64 combinations.
4. At Act II's end, the reader returns by navigating to a **numbered paragraph**, not a physical page: **“Turn to paragraph 475”** is the working complete-answer instruction. Riddle-like introduction plus very clear simple steps, worked examples (which may reveal 475), optional notes/alphabet chart. Accessibility and enjoyment are more important than guarding the answer.
5. An alternative **same hidden cheat code** can be found in a limited secret route in each regional Act II; using it discards normal clues and gives an Act III advantage. A **guaranteed fallback** for readers without the answer enters Act III with a disadvantage. All three routes eventually reconverge. Specific cheat destination, advantage, fallback cost and final paragraph numbering are OPEN.
6. Initial wrist-mark choice remains independent of all three common-Act-I clue occasions; it still determines Act II region.
