## Mission: make the next step WRITEABLE
The owner wants to start creating a real manuscript without either writing a linear novel and bolting on choices afterward, or building a cumbersome fully specified graph before any prose exists.

Explore the **practical writing process** first, not new lore. Cover:

1. **Narrative design levels:** a short book-level dramatic spine and revelation budget; act/region arcs; scene/choice cards; independently identifiable narrative passages; reader-visible destinations. Which layer should be designed first? Which can evolve without breaking others?
2. **Branching as authorship:** how to write forks that differ in information, relationships, emotional stakes or consequences; where and why reconvergence works; how to keep shared Act I common without erasing decisions; how to write three genuinely distinct Act II experiences without tripling every line of work; how Act III accounts for each route.
3. **First practical deliverable:** propose a *small vertical slice*, preferably opening + the immediate three short explanations + shared Act I first encounter/choice + reconvergence, or another well-argued bounded segment. Explain what to draft first and why, without drafting unapproved chapters.
4. **Work-in-progress workflow:** recommend a small number of readable source files and a lightweight scene/edge/flag register. Define source-of-truth for scenes, choice wording, passages and numeric maps; distinguish authored narrative text from print layout. Show a simple example record or diagram only if it clarifies the writing process.
5. **Numerical navigation constraints:** when to allocate stable internal IDs; how/when to assign reader paragraph labels and page numbers; reserve protected destinations; how to prevent numerical processes from reaching unrevealed plot nodes; how to revise after prose moves; avoid mechanically interesting layouts that force awkward prose.
6. **Playtest and quality gates:** cheap graph validation plus actual human reading (blind runs), broken reference detection, unreachable material, unwanted repeated loops, lost wrist-mark memory, route-consistency checks, spoiler leakage, ability to reach region-specific evidence, narrative pacing, and whether the reader feels choices matter.
7. **Practical milestones and workload:** a realistic sequence of authoring passes from spine → scene cards → vertical slice → review → act/region batches → proofread/testing → layout. Consider how to collaborate with AI without letting it silently invent canon, collapse branches or generate huge unreviewed prose dumps.
8. **Physical print constraints:** readability and jump speed, spatially ascending passage labels, cross-references, illustrations, book/page numbering, draft manuscript vs typeset edition, avoiding specialist materials or required electronics.
