# W001 — Opening creative workshop

**Status: PREPARED, NOT STARTED**  
**Scope:** Human-led discovery and discussion, not automatic worldbuilding or fiction generation.  
**Prerequisite:** S000 scaffold reviewed/merged or the owner consciously chooses the bootstrap branch as the working baseline.  
**Invocation:** A fresh owner instruction, e.g. **"Run ZETA W001 — Opening Workshop."**

## Aim
Begin discovering ZETA as a compelling fictional experience and identify what Book One might be *about*. Nothing in this agenda authorizes chapters, canonization or a solution to the overarching mystery.

## Discussion agenda (questions, not answers)
1. **What kind of world is ZETA?** How strange, expansive, coherent, intimate, frightening or wondrous should it feel?
2. **What emotional and narrative experience should the first book provide?** Who is its reader, and what should they feel?
3. **Who might the protagonist be?** Explore genuine alternatives before choosing an identity or role.
4. **What makes leaving the Citadel meaningful?** Desire, conflict, cost, freedom, consequence, surprise.
5. **How should story, exploration, choice and mathematics balance?** What serves narrative rather than exposition?
6. **What must Book One show versus withhold?** What effects of the overarching mystery can be encountered without revealing its answer?

## Suggested session rhythm
- Open by reading [project state](../authoritative/STATE.md), [decisions](../authoritative/DECISIONS.md), [open questions](../authoritative/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md) and [canon policy](../authoritative/CANON_POLICY.md).
- Elicit the owner's inspirations, tonal boundaries, dislikes and reader ambitions; do not assume answers.
- For each selected topic, compare at least two genuinely different directions, each with narrative strengths and weaknesses.
- Synthesize tentative preferences as **PROVISIONAL**; ask before labeling anything **CANON**.
- Agree which questions deserve separate later workshops. Keep [mystery](../lore/OVERARCHING_MYSTERY.md) ultimate answer **OPEN**.
- After explicit decisions, update [World Bible](../lore/WORLD_BIBLE.md), [Book One](../story/BOOK_ONE.md), [revelation budget](../story/REVELATION_BUDGET.md), [decision register](../authoritative/DECISIONS.md) and [state](../authoritative/STATE.md) only as affected; log branch/commit/PR.

## Exit criteria
A clearer *creative direction* and a transparent register of accepted versus open alternatives. It is entirely valid to close with no new canon. Do **not** treat W001 as a commission to write the opening chapter, construct a full story graph, solve the mystery or choose technology.
