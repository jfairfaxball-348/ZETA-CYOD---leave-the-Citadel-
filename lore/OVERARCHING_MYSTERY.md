# ZETA — Overarching mystery (restricted design ledger)

**Underlying answer: OPEN (Q-0007). Do not solve it in the current workshop.**

## Approved narrative constraints
- Reach beyond any single mathematical problem (including RH, infinity or pi), region or symbol.
- Connect contrasting cultures/regions and support multiple works over time.
- Raise philosophical questions about mathematics, reality, intelligence, knowledge and consciousness.
- Permit clues and partial discoveries without a definitive explanation in Book One.
- Favor reader uncertainty that accepted truths may be incomplete or mistaken (DEC-0010).

## W002-approved foreshadowing architecture
**CANON (story design, DEC-0014/0015):** The first book ends with its local false-exile judgement challenged and an unmistakably strange, unexplained Act III incident that raises doubts about an antagonist's freedom of choice. Most antagonists believe their choices are their own; a few may suspect otherwise. Book One deliberately does NOT decide whether agency is really constrained.

**OPEN:** Determinism, externally imposed choices, simulation, an unknown higher structure or ordinary misunderstandings could all remain live readings. These possibilities are not equivalent to established facts or to each other.

**PROPOSAL — future π plot seed:** An extraordinarily powerful machine computes digits of π at an imagined rate; a discovery seems to link a reproducible non-accidental feature of those digits with decisions that have yet to occur. Its computation, inference, mechanism, cast and book placement are all OPEN. This seed must be developed independently of the film *Pi* (1998). Mathematical caution: π's digits are deterministic; finding an unusual finite digit string does not prove a simulation or foreknowledge. A stronger narrative test would require independently testable predictive structure, and even then its ultimate interpretation remains open.
