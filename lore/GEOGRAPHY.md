# ZETA — Geography and spatial relationships

## Approved Book One regional scaffold
**CANON (scope only) — DEC-0002/0005**

1. **Number-theory territory** — contains the Citadel, but the Citadel is only one part of the territory. Its *fantasy name is still OPEN*; **Arithen** (along with Primoria, Ordalis and the Prime Marches) is a PROPOSAL. Outside-the-Citadel shared Act I starts here, but a true regional deep-dive is reserved for an Act II route.
2. **Topologia** — Book One Act II destination. Mathematical thematic association with topology is **PROVISIONAL**; appearance, environment and literal laws OPEN.
3. **The Combinatorium** — Book One Act II destination. Mathematical thematic association with combinatorics is **PROVISIONAL**; appearance, environment and literal laws OPEN.

**CANON (design direction) — DEC-0004:** Regions should feel *starkly different*, potentially like separate worlds, even while ZETA remains their overarching universe. No continuous terrestrial map or shared architecture is mandated.

## Travel / relational notes
- The protagonist leaves the Citadel at the beginning and travels through an initially shared Act I in the number-theory territory, before a delayed selection of one of the three regions.
- The finale returns to the Citadel; no specific route, distance, travel mechanism or border physics is approved.
- The formerly proposed 'Topologia as a country' and 'Combinatorium as a sky city' are **not canon** despite the names now being selected.
- Infinity-associated distant regions remain an illustrative PROPOSAL; no fourth Book One destination is implied.

**OPEN:** Relative position, scales, maps, transport, regional physics, state boundaries, settlements, languages, inhabitants and how transitions feel.
